# ClearSight V2.8 — Practical branch KRIs and management workflows

## Open the demo

Open **clearsight-v2-demo.html**. The standalone application opens at **Branch KRI returns** and requires no installation, CDN or network connection. The 60 individual HTML entry points use the adjacent `assets/` folder.

For one shared browser origin, run `python3 -m http.server 8080` and open `http://localhost:8080/clearsight-v2-demo.html`. On Windows use `py -3`. Browser policy can restrict file-origin storage and downloads; the interface identifies session-only persistence. Scenario data is dated **13 November 2026**, separately from this release date.

## Branch KRIs

The supplied Branch KRI workbook is mapped into **51 typed questions across eight operational sections**. Each branch/month has an accountable return with directorate, region, common source reference and CCO/CCM remarks.

Sections cover cash and counter operations, physical security, fire safety, premises and utilities, conduct and data protection, customer service, environment and community, and people and safety. Counts are whole numbers, NGN amounts retain integer-kobo precision, hours permit decimals, and Yes/No answers retain their polarity. Zero, unavailable and N/A are distinct.

Adverse observations capture what happened, impact, accountable owner, due date, corrective action, occurrence/inspection date, additional context and source/evidence references. Documents retain their exact linked version. A reference string is not presented as an uploaded original.

The replacement-card-printer question only applies when the original printer is faulty. Changing applicability preserves the earlier working answer without counting it while inapplicable. Validation catches impossible door counts, contradictory CCTV answers and armed surveillance with zero armed guards.

Independent reviewers accept or request corrections. Accepted corrections update reporting only after review; previous submissions and common decision receipts remain available. Open remediation tasks follow revised accountability with history. Closed episodes remain intact, and a corrected answer does not automatically certify action closure.

The source provides no approved appetite thresholds. Included rules are illustrative and configurable for future cycles. Context questions receive no invented favourable risk status. Legacy three-metric unit cycles remain accessible.

Continuous indicators now have investigation pages and dated, attributable observation history. Undated legacy samples are not assigned invented dates.

## Roles, permissions and escalation

**Settings → Roles & permissions** opens the role catalogue, granular permission matrix, organizational scope ceilings, scoped/dated assignments, independently reviewed direct rules, effective-access explanations, preparation/approval conflicts, access review and administrator protection.

The **Escalation matrix** supports triggers, priority, scope, timed levels, role recipients, fallbacks and stop conditions. Its simulator distinguishes calendar hours from WAT business hours and exposes unassigned recipients. This is policy and workflow simulation, not authenticated server authorization or a background delivery service.

## Inbox

The sidebar and header provide a recipient-specific inbox for mentions, program changes, quality defects, expiring evidence, approaching/overdue deadlines, approvals and escalation events. Detail pages include source context, discussions, acknowledgement, next steps and deep links.

Read/unread, archive/restore, snooze, source/category/priority filters and bulk operations persist per demo identity. Mandatory alerts cannot be disabled. KRI approval requests track their submission revision. Accepted exceptions stay actionable until their exact remediation is complete. Reading, acknowledging or archiving an alert does not close its source issue.

## Reports for directors and boards

Every report perspective adds practical management interpretation, accountable owners/dates and drillthrough sources. Native charts cover residual risk, loss concentration and occurrence, coverage and action age. The 51-question KRI report is grouped into eight sections; sections containing exceptions open first.

Board packs capture audience, meeting date, preparer, commentary and structured decisions. Coverage must be reviewed before publishing. Published packs freeze figures, source definitions, narrative context and charts. Loss occurrence windows, current risk posture and monthly KRI periods stay separate. Approved N/A is excluded from the applicable-answer denominator. KRI trends require equivalent definitions and the same reporting population.

## Review walkthrough

1. Open **Branch KRI returns → Enugu → Continue return**. Review the fire-drill and inverter exceptions. Explore all eight sections, then use **Review & submit**.
2. Open **Ikeja → Open return → Start correction**. Inspect accepted facts and action accountability; accepted reporting stays pinned until the correction is reviewed.
3. Use **Question library → Inspect rule**. Changes apply to future cycles only.
4. Use **Settings → Roles & permissions** to evaluate a person's access at a location, then simulate a missing-owner or weekend escalation.
5. Open **Inbox**, inspect a mention/source, reply in the local discussion, snooze an item or create a follow-up. Switch demo identity to see separate recipient history.
6. Open **Reports & Insights → Branch KRIs / Data confidence**, then **Prepare board pack**. Add an owned decision, acknowledge coverage and publish the frozen report.
