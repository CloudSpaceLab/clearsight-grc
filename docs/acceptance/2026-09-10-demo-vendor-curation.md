# Demo vendor curation

The source-only demo population uses reversible operator archive metadata, not relationship termination, vendor inactivation or Matter closure. Migration `000089_demo_record_archives` retains exact tenant, legal entity, record type, record ID, manifest, reason, operator and timestamp. Generated typed IDs enforce entity-bound foreign keys to vendor relationships or Matters. One restoration records its operator, time and reason; original archive metadata and historical receipts cannot be updated or deleted. A new archive after restoration creates another receipt.

The vendor register excludes actively archived relationships in its PostgreSQL query before search results are paginated. Exact authorized relationship reads retain original status, version and history. Archive metadata does not approve, terminate, complete or otherwise change a bank record. An internal `IncludeArchived` list option allows a reference installer to reconcile existing exact samples without recreating them. It is excluded from JSON and is not accepted by the HTTP handler.

The current Matter list and summary list exclude active Matter archives before pagination. Actor task reads apply the same exclusion to joined Matter lifecycle, Matter Action and Matter evidence-request work. Stored tasks, underlying actions and requests are unchanged. Exact authorized Matter reads remain available. Documents, Forms and Imports are not archived by this change.

There is no public archive command. A curation operator must verify nonproduction/demo deployment, take a recoverable backup, inspect exact sample IDs and dependencies, and provide the source manifest before writing this infrastructure ledger. The schema does not identify a deployment environment; enforcement belongs to the operator entry point. The migration creates an empty table and performs no curation. Other historical reads retain their existing permissions.

Required regression: archive the newest of two relationships; a one-item page returns the unarchived record without a false next page; exact read retains the archived record and denies another entity; operator reconciliation includes it; original archive metadata/deletion fail; attributed restoration restores the population without new business events.

The Matter regression includes two findings with a lifecycle task, action task and evidence-request task each. Archiving the higher-priority finding leaves the other finding in a one-item current/summary page and all three retained tasks in a three-item actor page. Exact history keeps its status and version; restoration makes all six tasks visible again.

The final focused PostgreSQL 18.4 smoke passed both `TestPostgresDemoMatterArchiveFiltersWorkBeforePagination` and `TestPostgresRelationshipTransactionReuseAndScope` in the separate `archive_scope_matter_regression_20260910` database. It exposed and corrected a restoration-trigger comparison of generated IDs before PostgreSQL recalculates them; immutable source type/ID fields remain compared. Local third-party, continuity, workflow, bank-reference, HTTP and schema-ownership tests passed in the PostgreSQL build. The reference-installer archive reuse regression also passed. No hosted business data was changed by this backend slice.
