# ClearSight business and demo presentation design

**Date:** 2026-08-25  
**Company:** Cloudspace Technologies Ltd  
**Artifact:** Premium PowerPoint business and product demonstration deck

## Communication job

By the end of the presentation, Nigerian bank executives and implementation stakeholders should understand why ClearSight is a credible operating system for continuing GRC work and agree to a focused demonstration or pilot discussion.

The presentation must establish that ClearSight keeps ongoing obligations current, directs issues and changes through governed authority, reuses evidence before asking people, and separates completed work from independently checked outcomes.

## Audience and purpose

The primary audience is CRO, CCO, CISO, Internal Audit, GRC leadership, transformation leadership, and senior implementation stakeholders at Nigerian banks.

The deck is a buyer-facing business and product demonstration artifact. It is not a legal opinion, investor pitch, detailed implementation statement of work, or exhaustive feature catalogue.

## Narrative approach

Use a 15-slide executive buyer and demonstration narrative. It should be more concise than the 23-slide FraudSniper proposal while retaining its premium proposal rhythm, visual confidence, and movement between business context, operating model, product proof, architecture, and implementation.

The deck follows this arc:

1. establish the product and its operating thesis;
2. show the cost and risk of repeatedly reconstructing compliance work;
3. explain the ClearSight Program and issue/change model;
4. prove the model through evidence, authority, closure, and product screens;
5. connect the product to Nigerian-bank reference journeys and bank-ready architecture;
6. close with a focused pilot decision.

## Slide sequence

1. **ClearSight GRC** — minimal title slide; continuous compliance and risk operating system for banks; Cloudspace Technologies Ltd attribution.
2. **Banks should not reconstruct the same compliance position for every deadline** — disconnected registers, evidence requests, email routing, and manual reports create repeated work and weak continuity.
3. **ClearSight maintains one evidence-backed operating record** — continuing Programs, approved sources, governed issues and changes, decisions, actions, and outcome checks.
4. **Programs keep ongoing obligations current** — requirements, applicability, safeguards, evidence, reviews, exceptions, deadlines, and current status.
5. **Issues and changes govern the work that interrupts continuity** — regulatory change, gaps, findings, requests, exceptions, incidents, decisions, remediation, and closure.
6. **Every status can be traced to source, evidence, authority, and outcome** — source to requirement to safeguard to evidence to decision/action to outcome check.
7. **Today gives each verified role one clear next action** — actor-scoped work, reason, owner, deadline, source, and immediate result.
8. **Existing evidence is searched before another person is asked** — source reuse, focused requests, exact missing facts, immutable submissions, and governed imports.
9. **Decisions follow current authority and segregation rules** — verified identity, tenant and legal-entity scope, responsibility, review, authorization, delegation, conflict, and maker-checker.
10. **Work closes only after the required outcome is checked** — task completion, upload, implementation, response, and verified outcome remain separate.
11. **Four Nigerian-bank reference journeys demonstrate the model end to end** — Nigeria data protection, regulatory change, protected regulator request, and verified finding remediation; clearly labelled reference data, not legal advice.
12. **The product makes governed work understandable at the point of action** — authentic product screens for executive work, Program status, issue/change handling, and evidence review.
13. **Bank-ready boundaries support controlled adoption** — modular architecture, approved-source integration levels, deterministic fallback, durable records, and explicit current production boundaries.
14. **A focused pilot should prove operating outcomes, not presentation claims** — select one Program and journey; measure active effort, evidence reuse, routing integrity, traceability, response preparation, and outcome verification.
15. **Choose the first Program to demonstrate** — close with the pilot discussion and the records, roles, and evidence needed for a credible working session.

## Visual system

Use the FraudSniper presentation as the layout and quality reference. Reuse selected source-slide structures rather than recreating a separate template. Preserve the reference deck's typography hierarchy, spacing discipline, footer rhythm, and clean wide-canvas composition.

Apply ClearSight's institutional visual language through controlled changes:

- deep navy for high-confidence framing and selected section moments;
- near-white for the majority of business content;
- cyan for active information and navigation concepts;
- violet for governance and configuration context;
- amber for pending review and attention;
- green only for supported current or passed outcome states;
- coral only for blocking gaps or failed outcomes.

Do not use an invented ClearSight logo, a logo placeholder, or a blank logo container. Use the product name as ordinary presentation typography and retain Cloudspace Technologies Ltd attribution.

## Graphical elements

Use authentic ClearSight product screens captured from the available demonstration environment. Do not generate fake interfaces or use obsolete screenshots as current-product proof.

Use a small number of native PowerPoint diagrams where a relationship is easier to understand visually:

- the Program and issue/change operating model;
- the source-to-outcome chain;
- the pilot progression.

Use restrained icons and simple evidence/status graphics inherited from the reference layout. Avoid generic stock photos, decorative AI imagery, dense dashboard collages, unexplained percentages, speculative ROI, and large feature grids.

## Copy standard

Visible copy must read like serious bank operating software and a credible executive proposal. It must be specific, direct, and grounded in documented product behavior.

- Prefer Programs, issues and changes, safeguards, evidence, approval route, and outcome check.
- Keep responsibility, ownership, review, challenge, authorization, signatory, and escalation distinct.
- Do not imply that an observation is a conclusion or that uploaded evidence proves sufficiency.
- Do not claim autonomous legal interpretation, compliance, approval, closure, or external representation.
- Label product targets as targets and sample records as reference data.
- Avoid slogans, product-review commentary, competitive chest-beating, and implementation vocabulary that does not help the bank audience.

Each slide must have one main claim and one dominant visual. Titles should communicate the claim rather than merely name a topic.

## Sources and accuracy

The primary product sources are the repository's README, interface contract, use-case catalogue, continuous-compliance operating model, differentiation specification, Nigerian-bank reference journeys, application architecture, implementation ledger, and acceptance tests.

Any external regulatory statement must be confirmed against an official current source and cited in speaker notes. Nigerian jurisdiction examples must include the official source and date and must be labelled as reference data, not legal advice.

All external assets and non-trivial external claims require a `[Sources]` block in speaker notes. Product claims derived from repository documentation should also be traceable in notes where practical.

## Deliverable and quality gates

Create one editable `.pptx` file. The final deck must:

- contain 15 slides unless content fit requires splitting one approved idea without adding a new narrative theme;
- preserve the selected FraudSniper template structures and edit inherited elements rather than placing a new design over them;
- contain no invented or placeholder logo;
- contain no unresolved placeholder text, empty structural placeholders, clipped text, unintended overlaps, broken connectors, or illegible imagery;
- use authentic product screens and clearly labelled sample/reference data;
- render every slide for full-size inspection;
- complete at least one fix-and-verify cycle;
- pass slide overflow, template fidelity, copy, and content-order checks before delivery.

The final presentation will be saved outside the temporary build directory and delivered as the only requested artifact.
