# Onboarding, Illustration and Autonomy Acceptance Tests

## Guided adoption

- a first-time reviewer receives the correct role-specific guide;
- the guide is skippable, resumable and versioned;
- urgent assigned work remains reachable without completing the guide;
- unavailable features are not referenced as actionable;
- keyboard, screen-reader, reduced-motion and 200% zoom journeys work;
- completion state is isolated by tenant, principal and guide version;
- no sensitive content is recorded in adoption telemetry.

## Illustration and empty states

- light and dark variants preserve contrast and meaning;
- illustration is decorative and status remains understandable without it;
- no-material-change, no-data, unauthorized and source-unavailable states are distinct;
- assets meet bundle and rendering budgets;
- empty states have one appropriate action or deliberately no action;
- expired invitations and protected contexts do not leak details through artwork or copy.

## Signal and drift

- duplicate signals are idempotent;
- evidence aging does not falsely assert control failure;
- expired evidence invalidates affected support according to policy;
- source degradation creates unknown/data-quality state rather than false non-compliance;
- routing gaps create blocking drift where material authority is required;
- failed verification reopens the treatment path;
- every drift retains source signal and affected subject.

## Continuous readiness

- readiness dimensions reconcile to active drift and Program state;
- no opaque aggregate score replaces the dimensions;
- current, aging, unknown, blocked and pending-human states remain separate;
- every action drills into a governed Program, Matter, Claim or configuration issue;
- point-in-time readiness can be reconstructed;
- stale projections expose freshness and cannot authorize material action.

## Governed automation

- simulation shows affected population and side effects;
- action class and authority are enforced outside AI prompts;
- blast-radius limit blocks excessive execution;
- retry is idempotent;
- partial failure is explicit and recoverable;
- kill switch stops new execution without corrupting completed work;
- external success is implementation evidence, not verified outcome;
- policy expiry stops automation pending reauthorization.
