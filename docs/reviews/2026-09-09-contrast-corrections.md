## Stylesheet ownership and limitations

[Cleanup inventory](../evidence/2026-09-09-ui-corrections/contrast/style-cleanup.json) records all ten audited duplicate same-context rule groups: eight now have one owner and two unused groups were removed. Rules for the retired Forms property/quality/legacy/readiness panels were removed after source-use checks. Vendor/VDD/work-item native control rules were removed or scoped to retained native controls so migrated shared fields keep their own label, required mark, border and disabled styles. These are source cleanup counts, not a bundle-size claim.

Manual inspection concentrated on the finding families, their light/dark counterparts, selected/tinted surfaces, narrow controls, Responses disclosures, recovery and document access. Capturing a screenshot does not mean every pixel was manually inspected.

The 168-state aggregate retains **3,916 axe incomplete node occurrences**, **5,364 supplemental incomplete readings** and **252 boundary readings marked REVIEW**. Counts include repeated components. Uncertain gradients, filters and group opacity remain unresolved; lower decorative borders are not classified as failures. The supplemental checker uses canvas color parsing and ancestor alpha compositing, with 8-bit sRGB rounding. It does not fully model overlapping siblings, pseudo-element paint, blur, all internal scrolling or position-dependent gradients. PASS means no confirmed finding within these checks and states, not proof that every rendered contrast pair passes.
