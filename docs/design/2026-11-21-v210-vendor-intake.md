# Vendor relationship intake

The new intake has vendor, service and review steps. Values remain available when returning to an earlier step; focus moves to the step heading. Creation runs only from the review step and the returned relationship must match the submitted vendor, service, criticality and privacy role before success is shown. An unconfirmed result is treated as unknown and cannot be retried from that form.

Reference and prior baseline: `docs/design/decisions/2026-11-20-v210-vendor-intake.md`. Sample evidence is retained in `docs/evidence/2026-11-21-v210-vendor-intake/`. A scripted browser check covered service and review at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768 and 390 pixels, focus on step transitions, back navigation and horizontal overflow. No material create command was issued by the evidence script. These captures do not establish named participants, a persistent onboarding dossier, independent review or a live bank-authorized onboarding journey.
