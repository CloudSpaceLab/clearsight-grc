# Home reporting period

Home offers a start date and the current reporting end date returned by the existing source contract. Applying the selection sends the same dates to the authorized metrics and oversight queries. The view rejects mismatched scope, revision, period or missing period limits; current-posture measures remain explicitly identified as unaffected by the selected range. The backend does not support an arbitrary historical end date. No RCSA, KRI, compliance or document percentages are invented when their authorized populations are unavailable.

Baseline and source context: `docs/design/2026-11-20-home-insights.md` and `docs/design/2026-11-20-v210-clean-restart.md`. Sample captures at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768 and 390 pixels are in `docs/evidence/2026-11-20-v210-home-period/`; the inspected states had no horizontal overflow. They do not establish production data accuracy, historical-period behavior, full dashboard parity or screen acceptance. The focused test asserts both queries receive the identical selected dates and rejects inconsistent revisions and missing limits.
