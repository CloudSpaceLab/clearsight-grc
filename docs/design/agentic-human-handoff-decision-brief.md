# Agent-managed compliance human-handoff decision brief

## Product job and primary users

ClearSight continuously assembles compliance state from governed sources, evidence, deterministic rules and approved AI operators. A person should be interrupted only when policy requires human review, judgment, authority, evidence or outcome confirmation.

Primary repeated users are executives, Program owners, reviewers, independent challengers, authorizers, evidence respondents and administrators.

## Primary object and action

The primary human-facing object is an **Intervention Summary**: an actor-scoped read projection over canonical Programs, Matters, evidence and operator audit records.

It is not new authoritative state.

An intervention identifies:

- the exact human gate;
- the material conclusion or change;
- why it requires attention now;
- affected scope and evidence state;
- the prepared next step;
- the linked canonical record;
- optional governed recommendation, prepared-work receipt and verification plan when those records actually exist.

The primary action opens the exact governed record needed to review and act. It must not imply that opening a record itself approves, submits or executes anything.

## First useful outcome and repeated-use task

Within the first viewport, the signed-in user can identify the small set of items requiring their role and open the highest-priority one without scanning readiness dashboards, source inventories or complete Program records.

Repeated use should be:

```text
see human gate
→ read material conclusion
→ inspect prepared next step
→ open bounded decision context
→ act through the governed command
→ return to the queue
```

Quiet continuously checked work stays collapsed unless the user asks for deeper context.

## Structural pattern

### Level 0 — intervention queue

Compact rows show gate, state, material conclusion, scope, evidence, owner, deadline and prepared next step.

### Level 1 — current handoff

The opened Program or Matter first explains the current status reason or next handoff. Full catalogues do not open by default.

### Level 2 — review context

Requirements, evidence, facts, contradictions, decisions, actions and outcome checks are progressively disclosed in grouped sections.

### Level 3 — reconstruction

Canonical records, source lineage, imported material, audit history and operator receipts remain available through specialist detail and history surfaces.

## Required states and recovery

- loading without invented counts;
- live with linked action;
- live without a linked action, with explicit explanation;
- no assigned intervention;
- unavailable service;
- unknown governed baseline;
- stale or degraded source;
- contradiction;
- pending verification;
- submission review and receipt;
- optimistic conflict on the eventual governed command;
- AI/operator unavailable with deterministic/manual fallback.

Prepared-work or operator claims are omitted unless a substantiated receipt exists. Ordinary status data must never be relabelled as AI work.

## Desktop, tablet and mobile

Desktop uses a dense intervention row and progressively disclosed record detail. Tablet stacks the prepared next step below the material conclusion when needed.

Mobile replaces the two-column row with one focused stack. A later material decision/authorization flow should become a dedicated full-screen review rather than compressing a desktop inspector.

## Copy, authority and sensitive-data risks

- Use concrete actions and conclusions; avoid “AI found” or “automatically handled” unless an audited operator receipt supports the claim.
- Do not reduce confidence dimensions to an unexplained percentage.
- Do not expose protected record existence through intervention counts.
- Recommendation is not approval; implementation is not verified outcome.
- The visible action must match the real command or navigation effect.

## Illustration and motion

Populated Today, Programs, Work, Evidence and Configure states do not use decorative hero illustrations. Illustrations remain appropriate for onboarding, genuine empty states, education and completion.

Motion is limited to focus, expansion and context transitions with reduced-motion fallback.

## Acceptance evidence

Required rendered evidence:

- Today with three representative intervention classes;
- no assigned work;
- unknown readiness baseline;
- Program opened from Today with status reasons before full catalogue;
- Matter opened from Today with current handoff before history;
- Evidence with requests primary and source inventory collapsed;
- capture edit → review → submit → receipt;
- mobile Today and mobile capture review;
- keyboard, focus, 200% zoom and reduced motion.

The design succeeds when the first human gate is obvious and the complete governed record remains available without becoming the default reading burden.
