# 1. Positioning

ClearSight is:

> **A bank-first continuous compliance and risk operating system that keeps continuing Programs current, turns change and exception into governed Matters, assembles context from approved sources, asks only for missing proof, recommends the next action, and verifies the result.**

The product should be easier because it understands and reuses the institution’s own context—not because it removes governance or hides complexity.

---

# 2. The product moat

## 2.1 Programs instead of recurring reconstruction

ClearSight maintains continuing obligations, controls, evidence, reviews, exceptions, filings, and assurance as Programs.

The bank should not reconstruct NDPA, AML/CFT, PCI DSS, ISO, RCSA, resilience, or vendor compliance before each deadline.

## 2.2 Matters instead of disconnected registers

Regulatory changes, authority requests, findings, exceptions, incidents, breaches, losses, vendor deficiencies, and failed verification become typed Matters using one workflow substrate.

Registers remain views, not separate truth systems.

## 2.3 Source-led context assembly

ClearSight uses approved inventories and systems to assemble:

- legal entity and branch;
- Program and Requirement;
- application, system, asset, service, or channel;
- owner and reviewer;
- vendor and contract;
- customer, account, merchant, or terminal where permitted;
- project, change, ROPA, BIA, policy, and prior evidence.

Users should not re-enter information the bank already maintains.

## 2.4 Five-minute routine workflows

Routine work should normally complete in a few clear steps and less than five minutes of active effort.

Complex work should reach a clear saved next state within five minutes.

This is a measured product requirement, not a visual aspiration.

## 2.5 Minimum-question evidence

ClearSight searches existing authorized evidence, identifies exact unresolved facts, selects the best source, and asks the smallest useful question.

It avoids broad recurring questionnaires where targeted requests are possible.

## 2.6 Governed AI recommendations

AI prepares source-grounded first drafts for obligation extraction, applicability, mappings, requests, summaries, remediation, verification, policies, and response packages.

Users review structured proposals and exceptions rather than start from blank pages.

AI remains scoped, validated, approval-aware, and auditable.

## 2.7 Evidence-backed compliance state

Compliance is represented through interpretation, applicability, control design, implementation, evidence sufficiency, effectiveness, exceptions, assurance, deadlines, and source quality.

A manually selected RAG status or unexplained percentage is insufficient.

## 2.8 Outcome and response verification

Task completion, evidence upload, submission, implementation, regulator response, and verified outcome remain distinct.

A Matter closes according to a type-specific closure contract.

## 2.9 Institutional memory

ClearSight preserves what applied, what was known, what changed, which recommendation was made, who approved, what was done, what was submitted, and what result was observed.

# 3. Differentiation from traditional GRC suites

Traditional suites often provide broad configurable records and workflows but impose:

- module navigation;
- form-first configuration;
- repeated manual ownership and scope entry;
- static campaigns;
- large questionnaires;
- dashboard density;
- manual regulatory mapping;
- implementation completion mistaken for assurance.

ClearSight differentiates through:

- Programs and Matters;
- source-powered prefill;
- progressive integration;
- dynamic evidence requests;
- review by exception;
- five-minute active-effort budgets;
- outcome verification;
- protected authority handling;
- direct bank-language interaction.

---

# 4. Differentiation from lightweight compliance automation

Lightweight products demonstrate that compliance can be fast and approachable.

ClearSight retains directness while supporting:

- banks and multiple legal entities;
- authority and segregation of duties;
- protected customer and authority cases;
- source limitations and data quality;
- operational banking context;
- three-lines independence;
- examiner-grade lineage;
- continuing Programs beyond certification checklists;
- verified remediation.

ClearSight may use tools such as Probo for commodity execution while retaining institutional context, source authority, material decisions, and verification.

---

# 5. Differentiation from generic AI assistants

A generic assistant may summarize a regulation or spreadsheet but usually lacks:

- durable Programs and Matters;
- source authority;
- exact institutional scope;
- object-level authorization;
- approved inventories;
- temporal state;
- authority matrices;
- side-effect controls;
- evidence and closure policy.

ClearSight is not chat over GRC data.

AI is embedded as context assembly, prefill, recommendation, reconciliation, explanation, and drafting within governed workflows.

# 6. Bank-specific advantage

ClearSight uses bank context such as:

- channels and critical services;
- branches and legal entities;
- customers, accounts, merchants, and devices where permitted;
- transaction and settlement populations;
- applications and infrastructure;
- vendors and fourth parties;
- BIA dependencies and impact tolerances;
- authority requests and regulatory obligations;
- incidents, losses, complaints, and findings.

This enables a new circular or operational event to create the correct Program updates and Matters without manual cross-register reconstruction.

# 7. Unique mechanisms

## Context Assembly Package

A purpose-specific authorized package of source-backed facts, relationships, evidence, policy, history, uncertainty, and authority prepared before a user acts.

## Evidence Contract

Defines what proves a Requirement or Claim, from which sources, for which population and period, under what freshness and review rules.

## Minimum-Question Compiler

Asks only unresolved facts using the least burdensome approved response type.

## Recommendation Contract

Every recommendation includes sources, scope, assumptions, uncertainty, required authority, structured editable output, alternatives, and expected next state.

## Compliance State Compiler

Computes a multi-dimensional Program position from Requirements, controls, evidence, exceptions, assurance, deadlines, and source health.

## Closure Contract

Defines how each Matter type reaches verified closure, response completion, expiry, or accepted continuing state.

## Workflow-Efficiency Telemetry

Measures active effort, transitions, prefill, manual entry, recommendation usefulness, correction, abandonment, and resume time without collecting sensitive content.

# 8. Experience differentiation

## A calm first screen

Today shows only Programs and Matters requiring attention.

## Program before control wall

Users see the compliance position, gaps, evidence, upcoming work, and recent changes before a control inventory.

## One Matter workspace

Summary, evidence, decision, action, response, verification, and history remain together.

## No blank-page work where a grounded draft is possible

AI and deterministic services prepare useful structured recommendations.

## Correct component for the job

Cards for small attention queues, tables for populations and Requirements, comparisons for contradiction, timelines for history, step flows for imports and capture.

## Green is earned

Green means evidence-supported acceptable or verified state—not uploaded, submitted, assigned, or implemented.

# 9. Differentiation tests

A proposed feature should answer yes where relevant.

## Effort

- Does it reduce active user effort?
- Does it prefill known information?
- Does it reuse approved inventory or evidence?
- Can routine work finish within five minutes?
- Can complex work reach a safe saved next state quickly?
- Does AI remove blank-page work?

## Continuity

- Does it strengthen a Program rather than create a separate register?
- Does change create a typed Matter?
- Can one update flow to all derived views?

## Evidence and trust

- Are source authority, freshness, scope, and contradiction explicit?
- Is the request tied to an exact unresolved Claim?
- Does closure require appropriate evidence?

## Experience

- Is one next action obvious?
- Can the workflow remain in one coherent workspace?
- Does it use banking language?
- Does it remain usable without AI or a live integration?

## Product moat

- Could the feature exist unchanged in a generic form-based GRC product?
- Does it strengthen Programs, Matters, source-led prefill, minimum-question evidence, recommendations, or verification?
- Does it eliminate an existing manual reconstruction step?

# 10. Final standard

ClearSight wins when it:

- keeps continuing obligations current;
- turns change into the correct governed work;
- understands the institution well enough to prefill workflows;
- asks people less;
- gives reviewers useful grounded first drafts;
- preserves authority and evidence;
- verifies outcomes;
- lets routine users finish in a few minutes;
- remains defensible years later.

The product should not merely look futuristic. It should feel as though the bank’s compliance and risk work has become continuously prepared, minimally demanding, explicitly governed, and easier to complete.
