# Continuous Compliance and Governed Autonomy

ClearSight continuously maintains a defensible institutional position. It does not autonomously declare legal compliance or make material decisions.

## Core loop

```text
Observe authoritative and operational change
→ normalize a Signal
→ detect drift, expiry, contradiction or routing weakness
→ assess affected Programs, Matters, Claims and decisions
→ use existing evidence and precedent
→ prepare the smallest governed next step
→ resolve the responsible, reviewer and authority
→ act automatically only where policy permits
→ verify the outcome
→ update readiness and institutional memory
```

## 1. Compliance Signal Fabric

A Signal is a source-preserving observation that may change compliance or risk handling. Sources include:

- regulatory publications and amendments;
- evidence aging and expiry;
- source degradation;
- employee, role, owner and delegation changes;
- application, asset, vendor, branch and process changes;
- control and test failures;
- incidents, losses and complaints;
- failed verification;
- threshold and deadline events.

Signals are idempotent, versioned, attributable and distinguishable from incidents or approved conclusions.

## 2. Drift Detection

The Drift Engine compares the maintained baseline with current observations across:

- requirement and applicability;
- control design and implementation;
- evidence freshness, coverage and contradiction;
- source health;
- responsibility and authority coverage;
- deadlines and commitments;
- verified outcome.

Drift increases attention or uncertainty; it does not automatically exaggerate underlying risk.

## 3. Evidence Aging and Decay

Evidence moves through explicit states:

```text
current → aging → stale soon → stale → contradicted or superseded
```

Freshness depends on the Evidence Contract, risk velocity, population and source authority. Aging should create low-effort proactive refresh work before a Program becomes unsupported.

## 4. Routing Integrity

ClearSight continuously checks:

- missing owners, reviewers, authorizers or escalation owners;
- empty organizational positions;
- expired or circular delegation;
- self-approval and segregation-of-duties conflicts;
- ambiguous rules with equal priority;
- policy selectors that resolve to no principal;
- in-flight work assigned to inactive people;
- material decisions with no eligible authority.

A routing gap is first-class compliance drift and may block material action.

## 5. Continuous Readiness

Readiness is a decision-oriented state, not a vanity percentage. It separates:

- current and automatically maintained;
- aging evidence;
- at-risk or contradicted;
- unknown because of source weakness;
- blocked by routing or authority;
- pending human judgment;
- awaiting verification.

Every dimension drills into the source, Claim, Program or Matter requiring action.

## 6. Minimum-Intervention Compiler

When intervention is required, ClearSight:

1. retrieves current authorized context;
2. searches existing evidence;
3. identifies only unresolved facts;
4. reuses approved mappings and precedent;
5. prepares a structured recommendation or focused request;
6. resolves actors and authority;
7. presents one dominant next action per actor and workflow state.

## 7. Governed Automation Catalogue

Every automated capability is registered with:

- purpose and owner;
- action class;
- permitted data and scope;
- eligibility and confidence policy;
- blast-radius limit;
- reversibility and compensation;
- approval threshold;
- canary or staged activation;
- monitoring and kill switch;
- expiry and reauthorization;
- Verification Contract.

Autonomy levels:

- **observe** — collect and normalize;
- **analyze** — detect drift and propose;
- **prepare** — create drafts, requests or tasks;
- **execute reversible low-impact action** — only under explicit policy;
- **material decision or external representation** — human authorized.

## 8. Institutional Precedent Memory

ClearSight may reuse approved:

- interpretations and applicability decisions;
- source mappings;
- evidence contracts and accepted evidence patterns;
- routing choices;
- response-package structures;
- remediation and verification patterns.

Reuse is versioned and scope-aware. Precedent never silently becomes policy, and changed facts or source versions reopen review.

## 9. Failure and degraded operation

The continuous loop must remain useful when:

- a source is unavailable;
- AI is unavailable;
- a worker is delayed;
- a projection is stale;
- a role cannot resolve;
- evidence contradicts prior state.

Deterministic context remains visible, stale age is explicit, unsafe actions are blocked, and work remains resumable.

## Success measures

- time from meaningful change to governed detection;
- percentage of drift resolved before deadline or evidence expiry;
- human questions eliminated through source reuse;
- routing gaps detected before task creation;
- autonomous recommendations accepted, edited and rejected;
- automated actions later verified effective;
- missed drift and false materiality rates;
- readiness reconstruction at a selected historical time.
