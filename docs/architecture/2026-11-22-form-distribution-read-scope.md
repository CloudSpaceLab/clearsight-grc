# Form request read scope

Status: temporary containment; distribution read workflows are unavailable pending scoped repository reads.

The registered Form request list, exact detail and distribution response-revision list previously checked verified tenant and legal entity but did not include the person or subject visibility in their database predicates. These routes now return unavailable after identity and entity validation, before the repository is read. This also prevents the assignment recovery list from loading. Completed response reads and other routes are unchanged. The gate applies in production and demo; it must not be removed to restore a workflow until the scoped reads below are proven.

A replacement must filter by verified principal, exact tenant/entity and current subject visibility inside the indexed repository query before limits and keyset pagination. The list and detail must share the same visibility policy. The revision list must apply its own scoped response visibility, including exact distribution and submission links; visibility of a distribution alone must not grant access to all response revisions. Program and Matter access must respect restricted-subject grants, and third-party work and assessment origins must use their current workflow-specific read rules. Expired identity, missing subject, malformed scope, ambiguous tenant or entity and authority errors fail closed. A user-provided owner or subject filter can narrow results but cannot grant access.

The product specification currently states legal-entity-scoped distribution pagination but does not specify the complete individual visibility population. Determine and document the existing bank role, owner, named recipient, current route, delegation and read-all semantics before implementing; do not infer that the person who created the record retains access after subject or role revocation. Reuse existing access predicates where their semantics match. Memory adapters must provide equivalent scoped behavior or remain unavailable.

Acceptance requires real PostgreSQL fixtures with multiple tenants, entities and principals; permitted and restricted Program/Matter/vendor subjects; creator and non-creator actors; named recipients; revoked and delegated authority; malformed scope; third-party work and assessment links; list limits and keyset cursors; exact detail and revision reads; and authorization changes between pages. Verify filtering occurs before pagination and hidden records never alter visible counts or cursors. Test the actual HTTP routes and the existing assignment recovery path, including unavailable and retry states. Restore the three routes only after the policy and evidence are reviewed.

Distribution amendments remain a separate material command. The PostgreSQL amendment receipt is now assembled inside its transaction, so failure to read the resulting bundle rolls back its row, event and outbox. This does not establish transaction-time authority, session validation or safe unknown-commit recovery; do not expose amendment controls on that basis.
