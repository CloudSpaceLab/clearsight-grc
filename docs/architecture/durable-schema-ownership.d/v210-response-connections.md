The API-question proposal, approval and manual-pin tables below are reserved for the named form integration phase. Their routes and runtime use remain disabled pending the authority, lifecycle and capture checks in `docs/design/2026-11-20-api-question-protected-drafts.md`. Retention and partition decisions are release gates; the rows do not imply production readiness.
